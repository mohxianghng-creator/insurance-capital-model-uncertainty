The decision and the evidence boundary

The annual resource level is VaR at 99.5%. Economic capital above expected retained loss is reported separately. Public large-loss records support a transparent case study; they do not reconstruct a complete insurer or regulatory SCR.

The central trade-off is the cost of capital shortfalls versus excess capital. Predictive mixtures and conservative rules can move both outcomes. AIC weights, candidate cutoffs, prices and cost preferences remain stated assumptions.

A numerically resolved quantile is not a statistically calibrated capital policy. B=80 capital-rule comparisons remain exploratory because inner-refit sensitivity is material in some samples. Read coverage, precision, censoring and costs together. There is no automatic winning capital rule.

Bootstrap under-coverage remains visible in the two heavier-tail worlds. For the semi-Bayesian method, up to 66.0% of upper endpoints exceed the base grid; the heaviest-world median interval width is therefore unresolved. Nominal coverage alone does not establish a useful or stable interval.

Every known true quantile lies below the base numerical ceiling, so the coverage event remains identified even when an endpoint is censored above it; interval width then remains partially identified. The infinite marker denotes unresolved numerical capital. Semi-Bayesian credible intervals and bootstrap intervals have different interpretations. A nominal rate lying inside a Monte Carlo interval does not certify calibration.

5. Reproducibility and remaining work

The fresh study contains 1,500 decision samples across five worlds and two horizons, plus 600 coverage samples evaluated by both interval methods. Python 3.12.14; pinned dependencies and seeded independent process tasks. Expanded predictive quantiles are recomputed up to a ceiling of 2,097,152 and must pass a halved-step comparison within 0.5%. Source hashes and raw-data hashes are checked.

Coverage and decision experiments reuse matching T=11 data-sample IDs; the counts describe analysis cases rather than disjoint datasets across experiments. Inner-refit sensitivity also holds the training samples fixed.

python scripts/reproduce.py verify checks tests, current and empirical evidence, and the file inventory. python scripts/reproduce.py all rebuilds both empirical and synthetic studies; this is a substantial compute job.

  • All synthetic decision worlds use Poisson frequency and are anchored to one dataset.
  • Claim-level iid bootstrap does not reconstruct event clustering or changing exposure.
  • Candidate-set robustness cannot insure against every unseen tail regime.
  • Real pricing, actual portfolio data, independent challenge and an agreed decision objective remain necessary for institutional use.
